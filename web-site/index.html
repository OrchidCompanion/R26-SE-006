The Research Problem & Scope

Commercial greenhouses thrive.
Domestic orchids perish.

Orchids are among the most prized yet fragile houseplants worldwide. Over 80% of domestic growers struggle with species-specific care, improper light placement, misdiagnosed foliar damage, and unpredicted flowering cycles.

Current Research Gaps

Disconnected apps, generic data, and fatal misdiagnoses.

• Floral Dependency in Vision: Commercial plant identifiers rely strictly on open floral petals and labellum morphology. During non-blooming vegetative phases, these tools fail completely, leaving cultivators blind to species-specific requirements.

• Subjective Placement & Inaccurate Weather: Home growers rely on vague instructions like "bright indirect light". Apps pull regional city weather forecasts that ignore the real indoor window shelf or shaded veranda microclimate.

• Isolated Diagnostic Modalities: Foliar bacterial brown spot and black rot present visual symptoms identical to fertilizer chemical burns. Vision-only apps regularly prescribe toxic treatments without checking substrate NPK fertility.

• Static Cultivation Schedules: Traditional tools recommend static monthly calendar reminders without forecasting flower emergence or matching leaf physical development.

The OrchidCompanion Solution

Multimodal AI coupled with real-time edge IoT sensors.

OrchidCompanion establishes an edge-to-cloud precision horticulture platform specifically formulated for household environments. By combining specialized deep learning models with physical microclimate and substrate telemetry:

1. Vegetative Genus Identification: YOLOv8 Nano recognizes non-blooming vegetative structures (jointed cane nodes, monopodial crowns, pseudobulbs).

2. Quantitative Placement Guidance: ESP32-S3 burst telemetry (lux, °C, % RH) benchmarked against certified Sri Lanka agronomic standards.

3. Intelligent Bloom Forecasting: RF-DETR stage detection coupled with a Gradient Boosting Regressor predicts the flowering timeline window.

4. Multimodal Disease & NPK Fusion: Vision ensemble cross-referenced with live soil NPK readings eliminates nutrient-burn misdiagnoses and forbids phytotoxic chemical treatments.

Core Research Modules

Four specialized components.
One integrated platform.

Each research component tackles an independent subproblem, developed and validated by our four team members under a collaborative microservice architecture.

01 Rathnayake R. M. S. D. · IT22140616

Orchid Species Identification and Placement Guidance

Solves the inability of existing systems to classify orchids without flowers. Features a custom YOLOv8 Nano detector trained on non-blooming vegetative morphology (Dendrobium canes, Phalaenopsis crowns, Oncidium pseudobulbs). Simultaneously interfaces with an ESP32-S3 IoT node (DHT11 and BH1750 sensors) performing 60-second burst telemetry over WebSockets to benchmark ambient illumination, temperature, and humidity against certified agronomic standards from the Ministry of Agriculture, Central Province, Sri Lanka.

ESP32-S3 Node 1 YOLOv8 Nano BH1750 Lux Sensor DHT11 Sensor WebSockets & MQTT
02 Gamalath K. H. · IT22190598

Predict Blooming Time according to Plant Stage

Overcomes the unpredictability of flower spike emergence and blooming duration for Dendrobium orchids. Uses an advanced Roboflow Detection Transformer (RF-DETR) to localize and identify five consecutive developmental phases: Seedling, Vegetative, Mature Pseudobulb, Bud Formation, and Flowering. The visual stage is paired with aggregated historical microclimate telemetry (mean, min, max, std dev of temperature, humidity, and illumination) inside a tuned Gradient Boosting Regressor to predict stage duration and calendar bloom dates.

RF-DETR Transformer Gradient Boosting Regressor Multi-View Stage Input Microclimate Timeseries Bloom Calendar Output
03 Perera M. I. S. · IT22250124

Vision Based Orchid Disease Diagnosis and Care Support

Resolves the frequent misdiagnosis between pathogen infections and nutrient deficiencies. Employs a multi-model deep learning ensemble (Custom CNN, MobileNetV2, and NMS-free YOLO26) fused via Weighted Box Fusion (WBF) with IoU-based NMS for lesion localization and classification (Bacterial Brown Spot, Black Rot, Healthy). Incorporates Grad-CAM visual explainability and queries 7-day soil NPK telemetry to differentiate nutrient burns from active infections, prescribing tailored remedies while banning phytotoxic copper on Dendrobiums.

CNN + YOLO26 Ensemble Weighted Box Fusion Grad-CAM Heatmaps 7-Day NPK Cross-Ref Genus-Safe Treatments
04 Ranasinghe W. R. A. D. P. N. · IT22085726

IoT and Image Based Health Monitoring and Fertilizer Recommendation System

Replaces arbitrary fertilization schedules with empirical phenotyping and substrate sensing. Interrogates an industrial SN-3002-TR-NPK-N01 probe via RS-485 Modbus RTU on ESP32-S3 Node 2 to capture Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium in real time. Simultaneously processes leaf imagery using YOLOv11 instance segmentation and OpenCV with a reference coin for pixel-to-metric dimension extraction. A Random Forest classifier determines growth stage (Vegetative, Pre-Flowering, Matured) and calculates stage-adapted NPK fertilizer dosages.
